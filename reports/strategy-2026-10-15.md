## Daily Strategy Report: 2026-10-15

**Data caveat:** 17 Shorts and about 3 days of data. Retention and subscriber figures exist for only the 3 oldest Shorts, so changes stay moderate.

### What is working
- **Two Shorts are far ahead.** The phone infrared camera Short (tech) has 1,205 views and 13 likes. Null Island (geography) reached 1,025 views and 13 likes in one day. The next best is the USB cable Short at 421. Almost everything else sits at 100-240.
- **Both leaders give the viewer something concrete.** One asks you to try it on your own phone with a TV remote. The other says you have never set foot in the place and your apps send strangers there.
- **First retention numbers are good.** Spacebar trackpad holds 68.2% average view, Point Nemo 57.1%, ISS sunsets 49.2%. They gained 15, 12 and 13 subscribers on only 159-240 views each. That is roughly 94, 50 and 73 subs per 1,000 views, from a tiny sample.

### What is not working
- **Weak hooks get almost no views.** "Nobody tells you..." got 19 views, "You won't believe..." 8, "Why have you never closed an elevator door?" 6, and "You think you know... You don't." 2.
- **Space is the weakest category.** It ranges from 1 to 231 views, with no breakout.
- **Several of the newest Shorts have only a few views so far.** Judge them tomorrow, not today.

### Plan change
- Tech goes up to 1.5, geography up to 1.0, space down to 0.6.
- The writer rules now ban question hooks and hype openers.

### 3 numbers to watch
1. Views per day of the Shorts posted 10-14 and later.
2. Average view percentage on the next 10 Shorts, with 60% as the bar set by the spacebar Short.
3. Subscribers per 1,000 views.
