# Eutuber channel review and new plan (15 Oct 2026)

Based on 23 Shorts posted from 12 to 15 Oct. View counts come from YouTube as of 15 Oct, 21:16. YouTube Analytics (watch %, subscribers, traffic) only covers up to 12 Oct so far, because it lags 2 to 3 days.

## The one fix: stop posting the whole batch at once

| Batch | Posted | Shorts | Views |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Oct | spread over the day | 3 | 159, 177, 240 |
| 13 Oct | 2 in the morning, then later ones | 5 | 1,205, 237, 203, 19, 8 |
| 14 Oct morning | 5 at the same minute | 5 | 1,026, 421, 117, 6, 2 |
| 14 Oct night | 5 at the same minute | 5 | 22, 16, 6, 5, 1 |

When 5 Shorts go live at the same minute, YouTube tests one or two of them and the rest get almost nothing. The night batch on 14 Oct was also the second batch of 5 within 12 hours, and all five stalled. **From now on each Short in a batch goes public by itself at a different time** (22:00, then 13:00 and 18:00 the next day; with 5 also 09:00 and 16:00). You no longer have to switch them to Public in Studio.

## What is already working (keep it)

- **The Shorts feed is the engine.** 446 of 493 views on 12 Oct came from the Shorts feed, and 26 came from search. Most viewers are in India (344 of 493), aged 25 to 34.
- **Watch time is healthy.** Viewers watched 59% of each Short on average. The spacebar Short held 68%, Point Nemo 57% and the astronaut sunsets Short 49%.
- **The two breakouts share a hook style.** "Point your TV remote at your phone camera right now" (1,205 views) and "You have never set foot here, but your apps keep sending strangers to it" (1,026) both tie the fact to something in the viewer's own hand or phone, and you can test it immediately.
- **"Your X..." titles.** Most of the titles above 100 views start with "Your" or name a concrete object.

## What is not working (stop it)

- **Question and hype hooks:** "Why have you never closed an elevator door?" got 6 views, "You won't believe this, but glass rains sideways here" 8, "You think you know... You don't" 2, and "Nobody tells you..." 19. The writer is banned from these.
- **Abstract space facts with nothing to try or see.** Space Shorts are the weakest group, except Venus (237).
- **Subscribers: none yet.** YouTube reports 0 subscribers gained up to 12 Oct. The earlier automatic report that mentioned about 12 to 15 subscribers per Short was wrong, and the strategy prompt now forbids estimating figures that YouTube hasn't reported.

## The plan for the next 7 days

1. **3 Shorts a day,** each going public at its own time (22:00, 13:00 and 18:00 India time). Ask for 5 on any day you like, and the extra two go out at 09:00 and 16:00.
2. **Topic mix:** about 45% technology, 35% geography and 20% space. Space only gets made when there is something viewers can see or do (spot the ISS tonight, see a planet with the naked eye).
3. **Hooks:** open with an action ("Open your camera and...") or a personal twist ("You've never been here, but your phone has"). The writer stays on the rules above.
4. **Turning viewers into subscribers:** keep the end card, and turn the "try this on your phone" format into a numbered series ("Phone secret #1, #2...") that viewers will follow to see the next one.
5. **Checkpoint on 22 Oct:** by then YouTube's watch % and subscriber data for each Short will be in. If one category or series keeps winning on both views and subscribers, it gets a bigger share.

## Note on the hook scorer

The yt-script hook scorer gave the two breakout hooks 43 and 52, about the same as the flops (42 to 50). Its own README warns it can't predict a single channel's hits. So real channel data decides, and the score is only a sanity check.
