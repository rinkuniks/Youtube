# Growth plan: MrBeast-style tactics for Eutuber (16 Oct 2026)

## Where the channel stands (YouTube data, 16 Oct 19:28)

- 28 Shorts. 2,595 of about 2,800 views came from the Shorts feed, and 75 from search. India is by far the biggest country (1,399 views), followed by the UK, Germany, Indonesia and Brazil.
- **Breakouts:** Your Phone Camera Can See Light You Can't (1,213), Your Front Door Picks Your Country (1,203), Earth's 0,0 Point (1,062), Your Tears Won't Fall in Space (985). Space can win too when the fact is something you can picture yourself doing.
- **Watch %:** where YouTube has reported it, most Shorts hold 46 to 62% of viewers. Venus and 2 Miles Apart are replayed (over 100%).
- **The weak spot is engagement.** Likes run about 1% of views, there's 1 comment across the whole channel, and **1 subscriber** so far (from the phone camera Short). People watch, but they don't act.

## What MrBeast's team optimises (from his leaked production guide)

Source: the "How to Succeed in MrBeast Production" onboarding document as reported by Creator Handbook (Sept 2024).

1. **CTR comes first.** The idea and title must make you click. The guide's example: "I Survived 50 Hours in Ketchup" beats "I Spent 50 Hours...". The more extreme the framing, the better.
2. **The opening is where viewers leave.** Deliver the wow immediately; don't build up to it.
3. **No dull moments.** Every beat must be new and escalate.
4. **Wow factor.** Do what other creators can't, so viewers remember you.
5. **Keep the promise.** The video must deliver exactly what the title promised.

## What is now built into every Short (from the next run)

| Tactic | How it shows up in the video |
|---|---|
| Instant hook | The first frame shows the headline fully (no fade-in), so it also works as the feed thumbnail |
| Escalation | Script rule: each fact must be bigger or stranger than the one before it |
| Extreme but true titles | Script rule: strongest honest framing, under 50 characters, no questions |
| Like prompt | A small "LIKE if you didn't know this" sticker slides in once, just past the middle |
| Comment prompt | The end card asks one specific question with a one-word answer ("Would you live here? Yes or no"); it's also added to the description |
| Subscribe prompt | Same end card, with an animated Subscribe button that turns into "Subscribed" |
| Posting | Each Short goes public at its own time (22:00, 13:00, 18:00, 09:00, 16:00) instead of all at once |

## What stays the same

- Open with something viewers can try on their own phone or picture themselves doing. No question or hype hooks.
- Topic mix stays data-driven through the daily strategy (technology, geography and space).
- No invented numbers in scripts or reports.

## Not done (needs your OK)
