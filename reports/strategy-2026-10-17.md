## Daily report, 17 Oct

The data is still thin: 31 Shorts over 5 days, with retention and subscribers lagging. The changes below are therefore moderate.

### What is working
- **Plain second-person hooks about your own life or body.** Front Door has 1,216 views, Tears in Space 1,014, Null Island 1,085, Gibraltar red light 807, Candle Flame 684 and the GPS-through-Canada Short 620.
- **Geography is the strongest category.** Its median is about 240 views, against about 120 for space and about 14 for tech.
- **Tech only works as a 10-second test any phone can do.** The Infrared remote test has 1,213 views and the USB cable Short has 485. Every obscure-sensor or Apple-only tech Short stayed at 41 views or fewer, and most under 15 (magnetometer, barometer, seismometer, back-tap, watch).

### What is not working
- **Weak hooks.** Questions (Elevator 7 views), "You think you know... You don't" (Alaska 5), "Nobody tells you" (Africa 21) and "You won't believe" (glass rain 9).
- **Abstract topics.** Bir Tawil has 8 views and the ISS-spotting Short has 1.

### Retention and subscribers
- Reported retention on the biggest Shorts is 47–59%: Point Nemo 58.7, Null Island 54.7, Infrared 52.6, USB 46.8.
- Only 3 Shorts have a subscriber in the file: Infrared, USB and Null Island, with 1 each.
- Front Door, Tears and Candle have no retention or subscriber data yet.
- India is 1,670 of 2,466 viewed country views (about 68%), and 3,590 of 3,839 views come from the Shorts feed.
