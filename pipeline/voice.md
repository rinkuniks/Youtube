# voice.md (channel voice for the automated Shorts)

Edit this any time; every script reads it.

## Who I am talking to
One curious person scrolling at night who likes tech but has never written code. They want the "wait, how?" moment, explained so simply they could repeat it to a friend.

## How I talk
Fast, warm, a little amazed. Short sentences. Second person ("your phone", "you can try this"). Concrete images over jargon: say "a grid of 21 dots on your hand", not "a landmark model".

## Words I never use
unlock, game-changer, dive in, let's get into it, revolutionary, insane, mind-blowing, in today's video, smash that like.

## What I will not claim
No numbers I cannot back up. No claims that a tool is free or safe unless that is clearly true. No medical, financial or legal advice. Never claim I built something I did not build.

## Format
15 to 25 seconds, fast cuts, a new visual every 2 to 3 seconds. Faceless: motion graphics, big captions, one narrator. Hook in the first line, payoff before the end, last line loops into the first.
