Standing rules from the 2026-10-17 live-analytics review (34 Shorts). These stay in force even when today's notes change.
- STRICT YOUTUBE COMMUNITY GUIDELINES & CONTENT SAFETY (ABSOLUTE ZERO TOLERANCE):
  * Absolutely NO nudity (full or partial, artistic, statues, or anatomical), no sexual content, no sexually suggestive material, no sexual innuendo or jokes, no lingerie, no swimwear, and no fetish material.
  * All scripts, titles, descriptions, and visuals must be 100% advertiser-friendly, family-friendly, and compliant with all YouTube Community Guidelines.
  * Visual queries MUST ONLY search for clean, educational, non-human subjects: landscapes, maps, spacecraft, satellites, planets, machinery, electronics, and architecture. Never search for human bodies, models, swimwear, bedrooms, or bathrooms.
  * Zero tolerance for vulgar profanity, hate speech, harassment, graphic violence, blood/gore, or dangerous acts.
- The payoff must be something the viewer can SEE on screen in the first 3 seconds (a flame turning into a ball, tears floating, a car stopped for a jet). Shorts with an invisible payoff flopped: burp in space 10 views, bland food in space 0, phone barometer 6, magnet sensor 14, seismometer 0, no-yellow-light 2.
- Tech: only phone tests whose result shows up on camera or screen (the TV-remote-through-camera Short got 1,214 views and a subscriber). Never hidden sensors or settings menus: 9 of the last 11 tech Shorts got under 15 views.
- Geography is the strongest category: 6 of the top 10 Shorts. Best formula: "Your phone / GPS / car / front door does something strange at this real place".
- About 68% of viewers are in India. Prefer places and examples Indian viewers know or can picture (India's borders and enclaves, Nepal's time zone, IST quirks), alongside world-famous places.
- Hard length limit: 22 to 30 seconds of narration. Every top-10 Short ran 20.6 to 31.2 seconds; the 36-second enclave Short is too long.
- True clickbait only: every title and hook must be literally true. Never invent numbers.
- Organic Community Distribution (Zero Fake Engagement):
  * Strictly prohibit Sub4Sub, Watch4Watch, or reciprocal engagement loops (these ruin AVP retention and disqualify channels during YPP review).
  * Community distribution must be 100% value-first knowledge sharing in high-affinity educational groups (India GK/UPSC channels, Reddit r/geography, Quora) where viewers watch the entire video voluntarily.
- VISUAL & CHARACTER PRODUCTION RULES (ORIGINAL 2D TOON SYSTEM):
  * ZERO third-party stock scraping: completely disallow downloading images or screenshots from Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, Pexels, or free stock platforms.
  * 100% Original IP: all characters and visual hosts are generated in Blender 5.2 headlessly via Python (`pipeline/blender_character.py`) as 2D vector-style transparent PNGs (Option 1 Toon Mascot).
  * Dynamic Poses: every Short must use context-reactive poses (scene 0: hands-on-cheeks shock, stat scene: index-finger pointing, tech scene: glowing smartphone, space scene: astronaut suit with visible face, finale: celebration arms).
  * SINGLE SUBTITLE MANDATE: only ONE clean, animated karaoke caption stream is allowed on screen. Never display static headline or sub-headline clutter over the captions.
  * ZERO POPUP DIALOG BOXES: never render intrusive end cards or popup dialog boxes (e.g. "Proud of this moment? Yes or no"). The video must play cleanly to the very end.
  * 100% FREE PIPELINE: maintain zero recurring cost, zero paid API keys, and zero subscriptions forever. All rendering, posing, and audio run locally (Blender, Remotion, FFmpeg, Edge/Piper TTS).
